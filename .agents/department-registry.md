# Department Registry

This registry tracks the active departments and their responsibilities within the Intvar Automation project.

## Development
- **Directory:** `.agents/skills/website-developer/`
- **Scope:** Responsible for building, styling, and animating the Next.js 3D website.

## SEO
- **Directory:** `.agents/skills/seo/`
- **Scope:** Responsible for technical auditing, schema generation, content briefs, programmatic expansion, and keyword strategies to generate B2B leads.

## Copywriting
- **Directory:** `.agents/skills/copywriting/`
- **Scope:** Responsible for ad copy, email sequences, case studies, and content repurposing aligned with Intvar's minimal brand voice.

## Analytics
- **Directory:** `.agents/skills/analytics/`
- **Scope:** Responsible for generating internal performance metrics, client reports, A/B testing, and GA4 explorations.

## Marketing
- **Directory:** `.agents/skills/marketing/`
- **Scope:** Responsible for paid media optimization, landing page CRO, competitor teardowns, and SEO/PPC synergy.
