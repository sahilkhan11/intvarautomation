# PPC-SEO Overlap Analyzer

## Department
Marketing

## Purpose
Analyze both Google Ads and Google Search Console data simultaneously to optimize total search real estate. The goal is to stop paying for clicks on terms where Intvar already ranks #1 organically, and boost bids on high-value terms where organic presence is weak.

## Responsibilities
1. Ingest GSC Query data and Google Ads Search Term data.
2. Map organic rankings against paid impression share and CPA.
3. Identify "Waste": Paying for ads on branded terms or #1 ranked terms.
4. Identify "Gaps": High organic conversion terms with low ad coverage.
5. Recommend budget reallocation strategies.

## Inputs
1. **GSC Export**: Organic search data.
2. **Google Ads Export**: Paid search data.

## Outputs
- **Overlap Strategy Report**: Specific recommendations for bid adjustments and SEO focus areas.
