# A New Dimension

The web is no longer just flat documents. With the rise of WebGL and frameworks like **React Three Fiber**, we are seeing a massive shift towards immersive 3D experiences. 

Here are some reasons why this matters:
1. Higher engagement and longer session times
2. Unforgettable brand storytelling
3. Advanced visualization of products

## Getting Started

If you want to start building 3D experiences on the web, you should look into `three.js`. It's the standard library for WebGL. From there, if you use React, `react-three-fiber` is a must-have.

> "The web is a canvas, and 3D is our new brush."
